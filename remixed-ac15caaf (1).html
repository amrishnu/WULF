The WULF Anatomy

The core of the WULF EV is its revolutionary Kinetic Recovery System. Unlike standard EVs that only rely on regenerative braking, the WULF harvests energy from the wind itself.

🌪️ Side-Panel Intake Turbines

Strategically placed along the "waist" of the car where airflow is most compressed. As the car moves, air is forced through internal micro-turbines.

⚡ Energy Transformation

These turbines spin at high RPMs, converting air resistance (drag) into supplemental electricity that powers your journey.

🔄 Bypass Duct System

A specialized internal duct prevents the turbines from creating excessive drag, ensuring energy gained is greater than energy lost to wind resistance.

🔋 Buffer Battery Floor

A solid-state battery layer stores harvested energy instantly, extending the car's range by an estimated 15–20% compared to traditional EVs.

☀️ Solar Panel Integration

High-efficiency solar cells integrated into the roof capture sunlight, providing additional renewable energy while parked or driving.

🧠 Smart Energy Management

Advanced EMS prioritizes energy sources (solar, wind, regen) and optimizes power distribution for maximum efficiency and range.
